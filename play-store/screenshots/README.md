# Phone screenshots for Google Play

Capture the installed Android app on a real phone. Save at least two images here. Four portrait images give people a better view of the app.

Use these screens in this order:

1. Home page with the subject list.
2. A subject page with ready and planned topics.
3. Search results for a real topic.
4. A finished article, with its title and some explanation visible.

Keep the phone status bar and navigation bar if they appear in the capture. Do not add claims or controls that are not in the app. Avoid personal notifications in the images. Use PNG without transparency or JPEG. The release check will reject files with the wrong format or size. If your phone makes a very tall image, crop only the top or bottom so its height is no more than twice its width. Keep the app content clear.

The screenshots must show the same app version that you upload to Play. If a later update changes the layout, capture new screenshots.
